gavel Your rights under DPDP

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, you have significant control over your personal data. You have the right to access the data we hold, request corrections, and in certain circumstances, request erasure of your data.

You also have the right to nominate an individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity, and the right to a dedicated grievance redressal mechanism.
