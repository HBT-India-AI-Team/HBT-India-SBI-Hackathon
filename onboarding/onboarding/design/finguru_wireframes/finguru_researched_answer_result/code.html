What's the best strategy for a 25-year-old starting to invest with $500/month?

lightbulb Researched for you

For a 25-year-old with a long time horizon, investing $500/month should focus on maximizing growth through a diversified, low-cost approach. Here’s a typical strategy:

  • Maximize Employer Match: First, ensure you're getting the full match on your 401(k) if offered. That's free money.
  • Fund a Roth IRA: With the remaining amount, maxing out a Roth IRA (up to $7,000/year) is powerful because earnings grow tax-free.
  • Low-Cost Index Funds: Allocate investments heavily towards Broad Market Index Funds (like S&P 500 or Total Stock Market) for consistent long-term growth.
