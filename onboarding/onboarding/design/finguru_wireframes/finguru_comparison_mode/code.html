What are the current FD interest rates in India for a 1-year tenure, and how does it compare to a generic savings account?

Currently, major Indian banks like SBI offer around 6.80% p.a. on a 1-year Fixed Deposit for general citizens (up to 7.30% for senior citizens) [1].

In contrast, a standard generic savings account typically yields between 2.70% to 3.00% p.a. [2]. The FD provides a significantly higher, guaranteed return, though liquidity is locked for the tenure.

Sources:

  • [1] State Bank of India - Retail Domestic Term Deposits
  • [2] Reserve Bank of India - Savings Bank Interest Rates

Fixed deposit (FD) interest rates vary widely depending on the country, the bank, and the economic climate. For a 1-year tenure, rates typically range from 1% to 5% or sometimes higher in developing economies.

Savings accounts usually offer much lower rates, often between 0.1% and 2%. FDs are better for locking in a rate, while savings accounts offer better liquidity.
