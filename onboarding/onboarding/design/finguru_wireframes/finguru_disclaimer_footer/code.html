What's the main difference between an ETF and a mutual fund? I'm trying to decide where to put my savings.

smart_toy

Great question! Both are excellent ways to diversify, but they operate differently. Here is a quick breakdown:

show_chart Trading

ETFs trade like stocks throughout the day. Mutual funds trade only once at the end of the day at a set price.

account_balance Management

Mutual funds are often actively managed by professionals aiming to beat the market. ETFs usually passively track an index.
