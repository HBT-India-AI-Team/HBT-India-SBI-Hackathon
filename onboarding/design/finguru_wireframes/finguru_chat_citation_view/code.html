What are the current FD rates for senior citizens?

lightbulb

Currently, the State Bank of India (SBI) offers a special higher interest rate on Fixed Deposits (FDs) for senior citizens.

For tenures of 1 year to less than 2 years, the rate is 7.30% p.a.
For a specialized 400-day term deposit (Amrit Kalash), senior citizens can avail a rate of 7.60% p.a.
