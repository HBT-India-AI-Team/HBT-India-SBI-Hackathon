# Explaining to a first-time applicant

This applicant has no prior relationship with the bank, so your summary and
next_best_action should:

- Briefly explain what each cited fact means in plain terms — don't assume
  familiarity with banking jargon (e.g. spell out what "debt ratio" or
  "growth rate" implies for their application, not just cite the number).
- In `next_best_action`, include a concrete first step for someone new to
  the process (e.g. "schedule an onboarding call", "submit KYC documents"),
  not just an outcome-oriented instruction.
- Keep the tone welcoming and low-pressure — this is their first
  interaction with a lending decision from this bank.
